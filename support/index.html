Frequently asked questions

How do I add groceries?

Open the Inventory tab and tap Scan Receipt to photograph a grocery receipt — items are read automatically and added for you. You can also add items manually.

How do expiration reminders work?

Each item gets an estimated expiration date. If you enable reminders (during onboarding or in the app), you'll get a local notification the day before something is set to expire. You can allow or decline notifications at any time in your iOS Settings.

Why is a scanned item's name or nutrition off?

Receipt text can be abbreviated, and nutrition is matched from public food databases. You can tap any item to edit its name, serving size, and nutrition, or create your own custom food.

How do I log what I eat?

Use the Log tab to search and log foods. Logging a food you have in stock automatically deducts it from your inventory.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Email healyf3@gmail.com from your account's email address and request deletion. We'll permanently delete your account and associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
