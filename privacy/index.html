Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Spoiler Alert ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS app operated by Healy Fuess. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. By using Spoiler Alert you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider), and your display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google.

Profile & preferences

Information you enter to personalize the app, such as dietary preferences and calorie/macro goals.

Content you create

Data you add while using the app, including food inventory, food logs, meal plans, weight entries, food-waste logs, grocery purchases/spending, and expiration dates.

Receipt and food photos

When you scan a grocery receipt, the photo is sent to our AI processing provider to read the items on it. When you use recipe features, related text may be processed the same way. These images are used only to provide the feature (for example, extracting item names) and are not used for advertising. Camera and photo-library access is used only for features you initiate.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, expiration notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us for this feature.

What we do not collect

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the app (store your inventory, track nutrition, send the reminders you enable).
  • To process receipt photos into inventory items and provide recipe/nutrition features.
  • To maintain the security of your account and the service.

How your information is shared

We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed to provide it:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication (stores your account and the content you create).
  • Anthropic — processes receipt/recipe images and text to extract items and generate suggestions.
  • USDA FoodData Central and FatSecret — provide nutrition information; we send food names/queries, not your personal account data.
  • Google — only if you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Apple — app distribution and (optionally) push/local notification delivery on your device.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing healyf3@gmail.com from your account email. We will delete your data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted connections and a managed authentication provider) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Spoiler Alert is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email healyf3@gmail.com.
