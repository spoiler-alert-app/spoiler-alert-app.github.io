Spoiler Alert

Stop wasting food & money.

Your kitchen, organized.

Scan a grocery receipt and Spoiler Alert stocks your inventory for you, tracks freshness, reminds you before food expires, and logs your nutrition and spending.

  • 📷 Scan receipts — AI reads your groceries and adds them instantly
  • ⏰ Track freshness — fridge, freezer, pantry & counter, sorted by what expires next
  • 🔔 Expiry reminders — a heads-up the day before food expires
  • 🍽️ Log nutrition — calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber & sugar vs. your goals
  • 🗓️ Plan meals — map out days to your macro targets for meal prep
  • 💵 Track spending — see grocery costs trend over time
